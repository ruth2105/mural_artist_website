## 📋 STEP-BY-STEP: ADDING NEW ARTWORK

### **Step 1: Log In to Your Admin Dashboard**

1. **Open your browser** (Chrome, Safari, etc.)
2. **Go to:** `https://yourwebsite.com/admin/login`
3. **Enter:**
   - Email: Your admin email
   - Password: Your admin password
4. **Click "Sign In"**

✅ You'll see your dashboard with statistics and quick actions.

---

### **Step 2: Click "Add New Artwork"**

You'll see a big button that says:
```
➕ Add New Artwork
```

**Click it!**

---

### **Step 3: Upload Your Image**

You'll see a box that says "Upload Image"

**Three ways to add your image:**

**Option A: Drag & Drop**
1. Find the image on your computer/phone
2. Drag it to the upload box
3. Drop it
4. ✅ Image appears!

**Option B: Click to Browse**
1. Click the upload box
2. Choose image from your files
3. Select it
4. ✅ Image uploads!

**Option C: From Phone**
1. Take photo with your phone
2. Click upload box
3. Select "Camera" or "Photos"
4. Choose/take image
5. ✅ Image uploads!

**Image Requirements:**
- Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Max size: 10MB
- Recommendation: Take clear, well-lit photos

---

### **Step 4: Fill in the Details**

You'll see a form with these fields:

#### **Title*** (Required)
```
Example: "Addis Cafe Wall Mural"
```
What you call this project.

#### **Category*** (Required)
Choose from dropdown:
- **Murals** - Wall paintings, large-scale art
- **Small Projects** - Logos, small designs
- **Major Projects** - Big branding jobs
- **Collaborations** - Projects with other artists
- **Logo & Branding** - Brand identity work

#### **Description** (Optional but recommended)
```
Example: "Large-scale mural celebrating Ethiopian coffee culture. 
Painted over 3 days at a specialty coffee shop in Bole."
```
Tell the story of this project!

#### **Ownership Type**
- **Individual** - You did this alone
- **Collaborative** - You worked with others

#### **Project Year** (Optional)
```
Example: 2024
```
When you completed it.

#### **Location** (Optional)
```
Example: "Addis Ababa, Ethiopia"
```
Where the project is located.

#### **Video URL** (Optional)
```
Example: https://www.tiktok.com/@masterstvo24/video/1234567890
```
Link to time-lapse or process video.

**How to get video URL:**
1. Open TikTok/YouTube
2. Find your video
3. Click "Share" → "Copy link"
4. Paste here!

#### **Featured Project?** (Optional)
☑️ Check this box if this is one of your best works
- Featured projects appear LARGER in the gallery
- Only feature 2-4 projects maximum

#### **Publication Status*** (Required)
Choose one:

**📝 Draft** = Not visible to public
- Save your work without publishing
- Preview privately
- Come back and edit later

**✅ Published** = Visible to everyone
- Appears on your website immediately
- People can see it in your gallery
- Use when ready to show!

---

### **Step 5: Save Your Artwork**

**Two buttons at the bottom:**

**"Create Artwork"** (Green button)
- Saves everything
- Publishes if you selected "Published"
- Takes you back to manage artworks

**"Cancel"** (Gray button)
- Discards changes
- Goes back without saving

**Click "Create Artwork" when ready!**

---

### **Step 6: Check Your Website!**

1. **Open new tab**
2. **Go to:** `https://yourwebsite.com`
3. **Scroll to gallery**
4. **See your new artwork!** 🎉

**If you published it, it's LIVE immediately!**

## 📝 DRAFT vs PUBLISHED

### **Use DRAFT When:**
- ✅ You want to save work-in-progress
- ✅ You're not sure about the description yet
- ✅ You want to preview privately first
- ✅ Client hasn't approved yet

### **Use PUBLISHED When:**
- ✅ Ready to show to the world
- ✅ Want it in your public gallery
- ✅ Project is complete
- ✅ Information is correct

### **Toggle Status:**
In "Manage Artworks", click the status badge:
- Click **📝 Draft** → Changes to **✅ Published**
- Click **✅ Published** → Changes to **📝 Draft**

**Instant update!**

## 🎨 BEST PRACTICES

### **Taking Photos of Your Artwork:**

**For Murals:**
1. ✅ Take photo straight-on (not at angle)
2. ✅ Good lighting (daytime or bright lights)
3. ✅ Show full mural if possible
4. ✅ Take multiple angles
5. ✅ Include some context (building, people)

**For Logos/Designs:**
1. ✅ Clean, flat background
2. ✅ High resolution
3. ✅ Show final version only
4. ✅ Remove watermarks or guides

**For Process Photos:**
1. ✅ Before and after
2. ✅ You painting (if available)
3. ✅ Close-up details

### **Writing Descriptions:**

**Good Description:**
```
"Three-story mural celebrating Ethiopian heritage. 
Features traditional coffee ceremony and cultural symbols. 
Completed in collaboration with local community center. 
Painted using weather-resistant exterior paint over 5 days."
```

**Why it's good:**
- Tells a story
- Mentions collaboration
- Shows scale ("three-story")
- Shares process ("5 days")

**Bad Description:**
```
"Mural."
```

**Why it's bad:**
- No context
- No story
- Doesn't engage viewer

### **Organizing Projects:**

**Categories:**
- **Murals** - Your large-scale wall work
- **Small Projects** - Quick jobs, small designs
- **Major Projects** - Big commercial work (Ephrata Coffee, Amibara)
- **Collaborations** - Team projects
- **Branding** - Logo and identity work

**Featured Projects:**
- Feature your BEST work only
- Recommend: 2-4 featured projects
- These appear larger in gallery

### **Mobile Tips:**

2. **Use phone browser** (Chrome, Safari)
3. **Everything works on mobile!**
4. **Portrait or landscape** - both work

### **Taking Photos on Phone:**

**Best Practice:**
1. Hold phone horizontally (landscape)
2. Step back to get full mural
3. Make sure it's in focus
4. Good lighting (not in shadow)
5. Steady hands (or use timer)
